# Lead-texting regression scenarios

Run these synthetic conversations against the lead playbook with fake delivery and staff alerts. Give the fixture a standard yard in a served ZIP unless the case specifies otherwise. Supply only the facts needed for that case. A quote follows the usual confirmation flow. [FAQ and price grids](faq-draft.md)

Check every customer turn for business voice, dog-waste terminology, ordinary hyphens and the appropriate next question. Only the first automated confirmation for a brand-new lead gets a blank line followed by `Reply STOP to opt out`. Later texts omit the footer. Verify the staff-review event on handoffs, including the reason, last incoming message, available contact information and any previous quote. Handoffs and opt-outs end the sales flow. Use fake transports for any delivery or provider operation. [Escalation rules](escalation-rules.md)
