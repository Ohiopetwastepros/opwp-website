# Dog-food customer hub and portal onboarding

The owner workspace has a customer information hub at `/admin/dog-food/customers/`. Its directory connects native customer records, addresses, recorded sales, payments, plans, exact Airtable source links and portal invitation outcomes. New sales in the owner workspace and Tony's `/food-sales/` use the same native sale service, catalog prices and 7.75% tax. Historical Airtable sale totals are retained.

“Add a new customer” opens a separate form with its own submit button. First and last name, email, mobile number, street address, city, state, ZIP, dog count, customer type, frequency, handoff preference, placement and SMS permission are required. Other placement instructions are required when selected; notes remain optional. The sale cannot create a contact inline or bypass these requirements. A saved customer is selected immediately for the sale. A definitive duplicate-email rejection permits correcting the form and submitting again. Uncertain outcomes retain the original payload and submission reference; stale edits require reloading the customer record before another save.

Contact, primary address, profile, idempotent command result and two invitation intents commit atomically. Repeated submission IDs return the same customer; changed content conflicts. Email uniqueness protects existing records. No credential or portal account is created on staff submission. Invitation email uses the existing temporary-password flow: 30-minute expiry, one use, verified email, mandatory customer-selected password. The text contains the portal URL and directs the customer to their email; it never contains a password. Existing accounts receive access instructions without changing their password.

The email-verified account links to the existing customer ID. Its complete saved profile and pickup/delivery preference are carried into the portal. Sales, receipts, address and portal history consequently share that ID. Staff cannot edit the owner hub, reset customer credentials, or establish ownership from matching names. Office edits use optimistic revision/contact guards; address corrections preserve addresses referenced by prior orders. Changing a verified portal email requires a separate customer-verification process.

Invitation email and SMS have independent durable states: queued, sending, accepted, suppressed, uncertain or cancelled. Provider acceptance is not delivery confirmation. Each channel is claimed once. Interrupted sends require office review, avoiding automatic duplicates after response loss. Queued messages can run immediately after submission and through the scheduled workflow. Existing SMS suppression stores are respected, and sends are bounded by the invitation budget. Unconfigured providers and exhausted channel budgets retain their queued messages without blocking the other channel. Contact imports do not queue messages or invite existing customers in bulk.

Migrations `0061` (count-backed staff sales and customer source links) and `0062` (customer profiles, command records and invitation intents) require separate production authorization under `AGENTS.md`. Apply both before publishing this release. Original private production SQL remains byte-verified. Public tests and previews use isolated fabricated contacts, stock and provider transports.
