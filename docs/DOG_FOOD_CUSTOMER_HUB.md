# Dog-food customer hub and portal onboarding

The owner workspace has a customer information hub at `/admin/dog-food/customers/`. Its directory connects native customer records, addresses, recorded sales, payments, plans, exact Airtable source links and portal invitation outcomes. New sales in the owner workspace and Tony's `/food-sales/` use the same native sale service, catalog prices and 7.75% tax. Historical Airtable sale totals are retained.

“Add a new customer” opens a separate form with its own submit button. First and last name, phone, street address, city, state, ZIP and pickup/delivery preference are required. Delivery needs actual placement instructions; pickup does not. Email, dog count, frequency, notes and SMS consent are optional. A saved customer can be used for staff sales immediately. A definitive duplicate-email rejection permits correcting the form and submitting again. Uncertain outcomes retain the original payload and submission reference; stale edits require reloading the customer record before another save.

Contact, primary address, profile and idempotent command result commit atomically. Repeated submission IDs return the same customer; changed content conflicts. Nonempty email uniqueness and possible phone/name duplicates protect existing records without merging identities. Saving or editing creates no invitation, credential, processor customer or charge. The office sends an invitation separately after confirming the exact customer email. The invitation binds to that customer ID and uses the temporary-password flow: 30-minute expiry, one use, verified email and a mandatory customer-selected password. An optional authorized text contains the portal URL, directs the customer to their email and never contains a password. Existing accounts receive access instructions without changing their password.

The email-verified account links to the existing customer ID. Its complete saved profile and pickup/delivery preference are carried into the portal. Sales, receipts, address and portal history consequently share that ID. Staff cannot edit the owner hub, reset customer credentials, or establish ownership from matching names. Office edits use optimistic revision/contact guards; address corrections preserve addresses referenced by prior orders. Changing a verified portal email requires a separate customer-verification process.

Invitation email and SMS have independent durable states: queued, sending, accepted, suppressed, uncertain or cancelled. Provider acceptance is not delivery confirmation. Each channel is claimed once. Interrupted sends require office review, avoiding automatic duplicates after response loss. Only explicitly requested invitations can run immediately or through the scheduled workflow; older profile-creation messages are cancelled. Existing SMS suppression stores are respected, and current permission must be bound to the recipient phone. Unconfigured providers and exhausted channel budgets retain their queued messages without blocking the other channel. Contact imports do not queue messages or invite existing customers in bulk.

Migrations `0061` and `0062` established the customer and invitation tables. Migration `0065` safely permits optional preferences and retains historical consent while adding explicit phone-bound evidence and invitation identity binding. Every production migration requires separate authorization under `AGENTS.md`. Original private production SQL remains byte-verified. Public tests and previews use isolated fabricated contacts, stock and provider transports.
